Village Gossip

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Village Gossip (in Slovak “Klebety v dedinke”, in German “Dorfgeflüster”, in Japanese “うわさの村”) is made by Patrik Dulovič, Slovakia. This policy explains what happens with your information when you play the game.

The short version

We do not collect or sell any personal data ourselves. Your game progress stays on your device. If you play without a subscription, the game shows ads from Google, and Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Game progress

Your stars, finished evenings, streak, hints and language are saved only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never sent to us. There are no user accounts.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players without a subscription see short ads between rumors. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about your device and the ads you see. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent using Google’s consent form; if you do not agree, you only see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice by reinstalling the app, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. Subscribers see no ads. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Subscriptions and hint packs are processed by Google through Google Play or by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns from the store whether your subscription is active and whether a hint pack was bought.

No analytics or tracking by us

The game contains no analytics tools of our own and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to delete all game progress from your device. To manage ad data held by Google, use your Google account and phone settings.

Your rights under GDPR

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for ads, Google is responsible; see Google’s privacy policy. If you have any questions, contact us.
