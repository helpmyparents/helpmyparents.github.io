Who should hear it, and who must never find out?

A cozy puzzle game for the trip home. Whisper a rumor to one neighbor and watch it travel the garden paths of Whisperton. Granny tells everyone, Grandpa tells no one, and the mayor believes nothing until he hears it twice.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

How to play

  1. Read today’s rumor and who must or mustn’t hear it.
  2. Tap a neighbor to whisper it to them.
  3. Close garden gates so it can’t reach the wrong house.
  4. Spread the rumor and watch it travel. Every neighbor has their own habits.

Two new evenings every day

Eight rumors per evening, each a little harder. About 20 minutes, perfect for the bus or train home.

A story in episodes

Follow a missing cake, a secret proposal and a stranger in a fur coat through two seasons.

One thumb, no sound needed

Big, clear pictures. Play standing on the tram, pause any time and continue later.

Works offline

No account and no internet needed. New evenings appear on their own every day.
