# HelpMyParents Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

HelpMyParents is made by Patrik Dulovič, Slovakia. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use the HelpMyParents app.

## The short version

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. Your guides and screenshots stay on your device.

## Guides and screenshots

Screenshots, photos and texts you add to a guide are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us. When you choose to share a guide, the app creates a PDF or pictures and hands them to the sharing app you pick, for example WhatsApp or e-mail. What happens after that is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

## Photos and camera

The app asks for access to your photos or camera only so you can add a picture to a guide. It only reads the pictures you choose.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only learns from the store whether your subscription is active.

## No accounts, ads or tracking

The app has no user accounts, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics or tracking tools.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

## Deleting your data

Delete a guide inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all guides from your device.

## Your rights under GDPR

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions, contact us.

## Contact

E-mail: parentshelpmy@gmail.com

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
