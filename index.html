Stop explaining the same thing twice.

Turn screenshots into step-by-step guides with big red arrows and simple words. Your parents follow them on their own, at their own pace.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

How it works

  1. Add a screenshot or a photo of the TV or computer screen.
  2. Tap where they should press. A big red arrow appears.
  3. Write one short sentence per step, in large letters.
  4. Send it as a PDF to print, or as pictures in WhatsApp, Messages or email.

Made for seniors

Extra-large type, one step per screen, huge Back and Next buttons.

Ready-made guides

Start from guides for video calls, answering the phone, bigger text or TV subtitles.

Private

Your guides stay on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Works offline

Make and show guides without internet. Print the PDF and keep it next to the TV.
